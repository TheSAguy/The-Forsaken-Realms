## The Forsaken Realms 1.28: Perks & Pointers

### New things to do

- **🏅 Five new Ascension perks:**
  - **Bounty Hunter:** bounties pay up to ×2.
  - **Legend Slayer:** your first win over each legend pays its whole card list plus a mythic from its deck.
  - **Treasure Seeker:** more map pieces, and treasures pay twice.
  - **Tournament Regular:** cheaper Inn entry fees, and a 3-0 run pays a rare (sometimes a mythic) from the event's set.
  - **Guild Favorite:** side quests pay +50% gold and shards, plus an extra card, and give you longer to finish them.
- **🎨 Every perk icon repainted** in one painted style.
- **💬 Tooltips:** hover over buttons and HUD items for a short explanation: the world map, New Game, Settings, towns, shops, the Inn, Ascendance, quests, the journal and map tokens. Turn them on or off in Settings > Show tooltips (Auto shows them on Easy and Normal and hides them on Hard and Insane).

- **🕯️ Caves and dungeons start with the HUD hidden** for a cleaner view (double-tap your avatar to toggle it). The world map keeps your choice.

### Fixes you'll notice

- **⏭️ End Turn "twitching":** the flickering, smeared End Turn caption is fixed.
- **🍺 Inn Drafts:** quitting during a final no longer crashes the Inn later, and the champion's prize is paid.
- **🪟 Empty map dialogs:** a game launched minimized (or alt-tabbed while loading) no longer leaves every map dialog blank and unclickable. This was the "quest freeze" some of you hit.
- **💎 Old-set boosters:** Legends, Alpha and Beta packs cost about 1,200 to 1,800 gold instead of about 59,000, and no pack can be sold back for a profit.
- **🧠 AI:**
  - Enemies now play their Incubate, Empower and Recruit cards.
  - An enemy combo that is actually killing you finishes its turn.
  - Stronger combo and timing play from the new Forge AI.
- **🏰 The Capitol's defense:** an attacking mage waiting at the Capitol (or a Capitol duel in progress) survives a save and quit.
- **🃏 Decks:** a deck below the minimum size warns you and sits out its duels, and losing an ante card you never owned costs nothing.
- **🔒 F4 (reload)** stays locked after you take loot while being chased or rob a guard, and a Capitol rune can't skip a duel's loot.
- **🖼️ Save pictures** show the game again, not the menu.
- **📱 Android:**
  - The first resource download times out cleanly instead of hanging.
  - The Inn's draft card sheets load in the background.
  - Card art is freed after every duel, won or lost.
  - The game shuts down more cleanly on exit.
- **🎲 A rare error at a duel's start** with cards that begin in play is fixed.

### Under the hood

- **Engine:** the newest Forge daily (the 11.09 build). Enemy decks are now built while the VS screen animates.
- **Better bug reports:** new log lines for trades, bank moves, Inn event repairs, text-drawing errors and Android exits.

**New game?** Not needed: everything in 1.28 works on your current save.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.28-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts
   itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
