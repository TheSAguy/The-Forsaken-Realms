## The Forsaken Realms — v1.17.1 — Old Foes, New Doors

This release replaces v1.17, which was online for half an hour. It carries everything v1.17 brought, with the win
streak's length now set by the difficulty.

Your v1.16 save loads as it is.

## New

- **Enemies learn your tricks:** beat the same enemy enough times in a row - **5 on Normal, 4 on Hard, 3 on
  Insane** - and every duel after that it starts with a Wastes in play, until it beats you once and its count starts
  over. One count per enemy, whichever deck it brings. Easy is spared, and Inn tournaments don't count. A notice
  tells you as the duel opens.
- **Archmages start every duel with a Wastes in play** - except in town, Ring City, capital and Capitol fights and
  Inn tournaments. An Archmage on a win streak starts with two.
- **New Game+ asks what comes along:** cards only; cards and the resources you carry (as before, the default); or
  cards, resources and a refund of everything invested in the towns and buildings you still hold - restore fees, the
  Capitol, each building at its price. A separate switch keeps or leaves your items and equipment. Research and
  blueprints start fresh as always.
- **New Game+ starts with exactly five coins** (1 Challenge, 1 Silver, 3 Bronze), and your roaming guards hand back
  their decks (into your deck slots) and their gear.
- **Well over a hundred dungeons, caves, castles and lairs have new map pictures:** the old front-view caves are now
  three-quarter-view caves of their own land, and many more places got new art - among them a new Eldrazi Prison.
- **The Bronze Coin button shows the gold it saves** on the "Card Lost" screen.

## Changed

- **Each kind of dungeon at its share of the map:** no more clusters of one kind (five Mind Slaver's Encampments near
  the start). Existing worlds rebalance once on load.
- MTG Arena's digital-only sets (Alchemy, the "A-" rebalanced cards, perpetual / seek / conjure) are out of shops,
  boosters and rewards, and enemy decks play basic lands in their place. Cards you already own stay.
- A town outside the center keeps one road into the Ring Cities - no more parallel roads near Orazca. Existing worlds
  are tidied once.
- Ground decoration lies under you, and the pieces that looked cut off are whole pictures.

## Fixed

- A quest sending you to a boss lair that had vanished pointed at nothing (Pest Control and Xira's Hive). The lair
  now comes back; a save already stuck is repaired on load.
- A line of water showed along some land borders (Blue especially).
- The White Tower church's skeleton walked outside its walls.
- Inn tournaments: the Simulate checkbox sits above the Prizes again after you have entered.
- **Android:** Buy Blueprint no longer covers the card shop's Refresh button, so you can re-roll without buying
  (thanks, Galon88). Also in portrait: the Armory's Manage Guards button no longer covers your gold, the Local Guards'
  hire prices fit their buttons, and the Inn's Simulate checkbox is at the top.

## Engine

Forge 2.0.16 (the 2026-10-01 daily).

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.17.1-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~210 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
