## The Forsaken Realms — v1.18 — Blame Vr01!

Your v1.17.1 save loads as it is.

## New

- **Notoriety:** win duels in a row - against anyone - and enemies start bringing **Walls**: a 0/1 from your 6th duel,
  growing to 3/6, and a second Wall from 25 wins in a row (the most is two 3/6 Walls). Which ranks bring them depends
  on the difficulty. Lose once and the streak starts over; Arena duels don't count.
- **A tougher AI:** more attacking mages (set by difficulty), a free opening wave of attack mages at the start of a
  game, an Archmage champion guarding every castle, and higher-ranked enemies earlier on Hard and Insane.
- **317 enemies redrawn** with new art and animations - they walk in four directions.

## Changed

- **Restoring a town costs more the bigger your realm:** 200 gold + 20 wood on Normal, then 50 gold and 10 wood more
  for every five towns you hold (scaled by difficulty).
- **Better loot:** rewards prefer cards you don't have yet, then your colors' sets, before paying gold.
- **Arena prizes:** each AI capital's Arena gives an item from its own themed set of eight; your Capitol's Arena
  draws from all of them.
- **Deep Caverns** appears one at a time instead of in every land.
- The Capitol has one Job Board for all its quests.

## Fixed

- The overworld ground could turn black after a long stay in a big cave (a graphics memory leak from the Manasight
  color dots).
- Cleaner roads: they end under their towns, and small pockets of ground closed in by roads are paved.
- White tribal towns offer their own quests again.

## Engine

Forge 2.0.16 (the 2026-10-04 daily).

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.18-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~210 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
