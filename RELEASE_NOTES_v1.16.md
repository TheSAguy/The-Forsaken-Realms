## The Forsaken Realms — v1.16 — Lairs & Legends

Your v1.15 save loads as it is.

## New

- **Dungeons breed monsters:** most creatures roaming the realm now come out of the dungeons and caves around you -
  each one's own inhabitants once you have been inside. The longer a dungeon stands, the more it sends. Clear the
  dungeons in an area and the land goes quiet, and the nearest town thanks you with +1 reputation. How much the land
  itself still spawns depends on whose land it is and how they feel about you: least on your own land and with your
  allies, most where you are at war. Quest creatures still come as usual.
- **The legend table:** legends appear only on the land of colors that are Unhappy or at War with you, rarely, the
  ones you have not met first. They hold their ground until you come close, start their duels with a Gemstone Mine,
  and pay half the gold and two thirds of the cards they used to.
- **56 new creatures,** twelve of them legends - among them Victor, a vampire Archmage - and new color + artifact
  decks.
- **A gentler start:** in your first week monsters start their duels with half their life (never below 20), in the
  second week three quarters (never below 25). Bosses, legends, Arena and Inn fights are unchanged.
- **Special equipment slots on your character:** blessings, captive souls, tokens and the Generous Ingot now show on
  the inventory and armory screens.
- **New art:** a picture of its own for nearly every dungeon and cave, new farm and Zedruu City pictures, and new
  ground decoration on every land.
- **Quests:** return-to-town quests, a 15-day limit, at most 10 at once; quest creatures also come on roads and on your
  own land.
- **Sets and shops:** a set now counts every card printed in it; color-named shops sell only their color; high-rarity
  shops moved out of the Common tier.
- **Inn tournaments** turn over weekly, take the current entry fee, and a win raises the town's reputation.
- **Towns** sit on cobblestone plazas, and approaching one lifts the fog around it again.
- **Speed-Up** now brings monsters as fast as it brings the days, up to a limit around you.
- **One Game Guide & FAQ**, now also as a web page (GAME_GUIDE.html), and a new welcome page with Discord and Guide
  buttons.

## Changed

- Losing a duel costs a percentage of your gold, with a minimum.
- Loot behind gates and locked doors is no longer guarded.
- Pickups and place markers stay hidden in the fog until you can see them.
- Your starting gear is worn from the start.

## Fixed

- "Bronze Coin" and "OK" no longer touch the screen edges on the lost-card screen at high resolutions.
- Loading an autosave put you back where you last left the world map instead of where you were.
- An old road could stop one tile short of a town you restored.
- Choosing the second utility slot or a second hand slot listed no items.
- A Master Blue Wizard could fail to load its deck.

## Engine

Forge 2.0.16 (the 2026-09-30 daily).
