## The Forsaken Realms — v1.13 — Heart of the Realm

Your Capitol now rises at the heart of the map, and dungeon treasure has to be earned. **Your v1.12 save carries
straight over**; Orazca and the new map layout arrive with your next new world.

## The two big ones

- **Orazca, the heart of the realm.** Where the campfire used to be, at the exact center of the star, there is now
  a ruined town. Restore it like any ruin - it is the **only place your Capitol can be raised** (still five towns,
  same cost). The Warden waits for you there, and the Homeward rune and every respawn take you home: to Orazca, and
  to your Capitol once it stands. *(New worlds.)*
- **Work for your loot.** Booster packs and chests in dungeons now have **their own guards**. A booster's guard
  holds its post and goes back to it after a chase. Take the loot while its guard still lives and it **hunts you
  across the map** - a little faster than you walk. Every map enemy now notices you within a couple of tiles. A
  dungeon you leave with loot still inside stays on the map until you come back for it; clear out its enemies and
  it fades sooner.

## Also new

- **The map:** icons sit squarely on their places, a place appears the moment its icon does, your own marker wears
  a pulsing gold ring, castles show the "not yet visited" magnifier, and towns and castles have clear ground around
  them. You enter a town by walking onto it, not by brushing past it.
- **Weeks run days 1-7.** The Arena, paydays and shop restocks all reset on day 8, 15, 22.
- **War:** raising your Capitol quietly draws an Archmage from every color, aimed at the side of your land that
  faces them. An attack that fails now leaves the town with its defender - it used to fall to neutral.
- A day passes 10% faster. Oversized creatures are capped at their rank's size, attacking mages keep their size
  after a reload, and nine creatures no longer walk backwards.
- New Game+: "Skip the introduction" no longer doubles your Challenge Coins, and the Jumpstart tournament and the
  Inn-tournament quest come once per player, not once per run.

## Fixed

- An AI town's guard icons showed through the fog and gave away towns you had not found.
- Fifteen dungeons could never be cleared, and patrols in dozens of maps walked through walls or out of their rooms.
- A dialog could leave the screen with no buttons to press (the "grey window"); it now recovers by itself.
- The research station listed editions that hold no cards.

## Engine

Forge's 2026-09-22 daily, up from the 09.18 daily of v1.12.

## 📱 Android

Shop, Armory and Arena buttons are no longer covered by cards or pushed off a phone screen, and the in-app updater
no longer offers you an older version.

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.13-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser/file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for — it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~210 MB — use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. Works alongside the official Forge app — different app, different data. Updating over any earlier version keeps
   your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically — you don't need to grab it yourself. Problems
or cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
