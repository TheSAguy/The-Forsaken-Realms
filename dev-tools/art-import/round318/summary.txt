6. ENGINE ASSUMPTIONS - CHECKED; NO CODE CHANGE NEEDED
------------------------------------------------------
- 4-direction regions: CharacterSprite.load() reads <Anim><Dir> for every sprite. The player's 8-direction hero goes
  through the same code. Diagonals use Left/Right, and "None" and the spawn facing use Right. EnemySprite,
  WorldStage and MapStage never override facing.
- No Attack/Hit/Death animations: setAnimation() returns early when the animation is missing, and
  getActionAnimationDuration() uses its fallback, the same as the 71 of the repo's 958 enemy atlases that already
  lack them (counted).
- Speed 0 (the behemoths): nothing divides by speed. forge-gui-mobile and forge-gui were searched; speed() is
  max(speed + modifier, 0).
  - The overworld chase does setLength(0), and moveBy(0,0) returns early (no NaN, the animation stays Idle).
  - Map steering clamps to speed() x delta; getMaxLinearSpeed is a fixed 500.
  - The 6 existing speed-0 entries all have spawnRate 0, so the behemoths are the first roaming ones. They spawn 1/6
    to 4/6 of a screen height from the player (on or near screen), stand still, and fade after the normal 20-s roamer
    lifetime unless fought.
  - If they should stay longer, set EnemyData "lifetime" (seconds) per enemy.
  - Two things would make one move: a cave placement's speedModifier, or a robbed loot guard's chase speed. Neither
    applies.
- Round-261 frame cap: the cropped cells keep every sprite under enemySpriteFrameCap (numbers in
  qa\test_qa_atlases.txt).
- The duel mirrors the enemy portrait to face the player, which assumes right-facing art. The head-end avatars are
  cut from the Right view, so they end up facing the player.
