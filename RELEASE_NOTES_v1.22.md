## The Forsaken Realms — v1.22 — Legends & Lairs

New places that open up as you level, new and redrawn enemies, and gear sets to collect. Let me know how it goes, and
please share your game logs with me: `%APPDATA%\ForsakenRealms\forge.log`.

**No new game needed:** your v1.21 save carries on, and the new places arrive in it as you level.

## New places

- Thanks to the Realm of Legends!
  - legend lairs, each with its own duel and rewards;
  - the Argivian Military Camp, with five shops and a duel with Myrel.
- About 60 new items come with them.

## Enemies

- Ordinary enemies now come as Apprentice, Adept or Master, each rank with its own deck (~1,060 enemies).
- Every boss, legend and Archmage deck has one stronger card (~1,040 enemies).
- New enemies:
  - Kenrith, Immortal King, an undead Kenrith who roams black land;
  - the Kraul Broodmother, a black-green Archmage;
  - three driders and six serpent-folk.
- Nearly 200 enemies redrawn:
  - about 160 as full characters: centaurs, aven, naga, gorgons, giants, demons, merfolk and more;
  - about 35 from new hand-made art: the praetors, the myr, Griselbrand, Krampus and more.

## Gear

- Gear sets: 15 sets of seven pieces, with bonuses at 3, 5 and 7 pieces worn. A Sets page shows each set once you own
  one of its pieces.

## Your hero

- Your hero grows with your level, from Apprentice size to Archmage size.

## The world

- The History replay: watch the whole realm from day one. Open it from the map, or from the Victory and Defeat screens.

## Engine and fixes

- Updated to the Forge 10.17 build.
- Fixes taken from official Forge's adventure content, including a deck that could hang a duel and the Skep's blank
  reward page.
- Baneful Cavern is fully walkable now.
- Several art fixes.

The full Game Guide & FAQ ships with the game (`GAME_GUIDE.html`).

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.22-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~390 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
