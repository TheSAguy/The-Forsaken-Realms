## The Forsaken Realms 1.29: Sharper Foes

### New challenges

- **🪞 Mirror Gear (Hard and Insane):**
  - A new checkbox on the New Game screen, on by default on Hard and Insane and not available on Easy and Normal.
  - Once you're dominating, Masters and Archmages come wearing a copy of your own gear: 1 of your worn items when you wear 3 or more, 2 when you wear 5 or more.
  - **Archmages:** on Insane at Notoriety 40+ and a 75%+ win ratio; on Hard at 50+ and 85%+.
  - **Masters:** on Insane at Notoriety 20+, 75%+, and 51%+ against that enemy; on Hard at 40+, 85%+ and 75%+ against it.
  - Fair items only (no fast mana, no Mana Shard items), never the same item twice. The copies count toward your Bounty, and the Alt plate shows them.
  - Existing saves have it off.
- **🧠 A smarter AI:**
  - Attacks when it has the advantage instead of sitting back.
  - Sees your team pumps and Boast before it blocks.
  - Holds a pump spell that would finish you next turn.
  - Stops wasting cards and mana: no haste creatures cast just to sit at home, the land goes down before a landfall spell, and a fight spell aims at the real threat.
  - Plays karoo lands and New Blood properly.
  - Enemy decks lost almost 5,000 cards the AI couldn't use.
- **🤝 Offer a draw in a stalled duel:** after 8 turns with no progress, the enemy accepts. Nothing is won or lost.
- **⚔️ The War Begins:** a short intro the first time one of your towns is attacked. Read it again any time from World Standings.

### Polish

- **🖼️ Pixel icons** on dialog buttons.
- **🆚 Long enemy names fit the VS screen**, in a cleaner font.
- **🐾 More enemies get real animations**, and the Blazeplate Orc gets a new VS portrait.
- **🪟 World Standings' dialogs** fit small windows.
- **🔒 A second copy of the game** warns "Already Running".

### Fixes

- **💥 The reward screen's auto-sell crash** is fixed.
- **🔤 A cause of garbled text in duels** is fixed.
- **📱 Android** no longer quits when you come back from the update installer.
- **🍺 An Inn or Arena enemy's item** that affects you now actually applies.
- **📜 Kiora's Boss Effect** says "end step".

### Under the hood

- **Engine:** the newest Forge daily (the 11.13 build). The Status page shows your "Active effects", your adventure
  folder is backed up automatically once per new version, Sealed events open faster, the AI no longer casts removal at
  an empty board, and many upstream fixes.

**New game?** Only for Mirror Gear, on Hard or Insane. Everything else in 1.29 works on your current save.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.29-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts
   itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
