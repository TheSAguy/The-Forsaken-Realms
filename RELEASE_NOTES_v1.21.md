## The Forsaken Realms — v1.21 — Faces & Fates

New looks for the world, a deeper climb for your hero, and two new places to wear your gear. Let me know how it goes,
and please share your game logs with me: `%APPDATA%\ForsakenRealms\forge.log`.

**New game recommended** to experience the full effect of all the changes. Your v1.20 save still loads and carries on:
a helm you wear moves to the new Head slot and pants to Legs on their own. Ascendance (leveling) comes only with a
character started on v1.20 or later, or a New Game+.

## New looks

- Some new artwork.
- Player portraits.

## Leveling (Ascendance)

- The cap is now 40, and levels 31-40 give perk choices. Level 35 is "Weavebinder" and level 40 is "Eternal".
- About 20 new perks, among them Opening Gambit, Gambler, Aura of Fear, Warmonger, Insight (4 choices), Pact of
  Embers, Challenger's Mark, Hunter's Ledger, Law of Stakes and Home Ground.
- The Level sheet now has descriptions and icons. Level-up rewards are richer, and leveling is slower on the harder
  difficulties.

## Equipment

- New Head and Legs slots. You can wear 6 main items at level 35 and 7 at level 40.
- Every item's art now matches its slot: boots look like boots, helms sit on the Head.

## World and challenge

- The Capitol Temple: offerings, honor by color, and spoils you collect.
- Spawns adapt to your last 50 duels: a strong record brings tougher enemies, a weak one eases off.
- Your last 50 duels also hand out help: a Clue and/or a Treasure for the enemy when you win 75% or more, or for you
  at 50% or less - the cards say why.
- Days pass 20% faster.
- Arena: a fresh bracket after a loss. Inn tournaments: one win per town per week.

## Quality of life and fixes

- A notices log, faster notices, and many playtest fixes.
- Recovered ante cards go back to every deck they left.

## Engine

- Updated to the Forge 10.14 build. It adds duel Undo, which TFR defaults to Single step; you can change it under
  Settings → "Undo in Duels".

The full Game Guide & FAQ ships with the game (`GAME_GUIDE.html`).

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.21-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~390 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
