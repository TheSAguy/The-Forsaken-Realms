## The Forsaken Realms — v1.20.1 — Android hotfix

**Android only.** On PC, stay on v1.20; there is nothing new to download.

## Fixed

- On Android, the Ascendance panel, the Info button, Speed-Up, Wait and the other controls The Forsaken Realms adds to
  the screen did not respond to a tap: the movement joystick opened under your finger instead. They work again, and
  tapping empty map still brings up the joystick. Thanks to Alikus for the report and the screenshot.

## Also in this build

- Every Ascendance reward has an icon on the level-up choices and the Level sheet, each rank of a perk grander than
  the last.
- New art for 100 enemies: merfolk, dwarves, goblins and mounted knights.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.20.1-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~300 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
