## The Forsaken Realms 1.30: A Fair Start

### A gentler first week

- **🛡️ No ante in your first week on Easy and Normal:** duels on days 1-7 risk no cards, and ante starts on day 8.
  Hard and Insane play for ante from the first duel, Play for Ante off is still off, and New Game+ has no grace week.
  Custom difficulty gets a "Weeks without ante" setting.
- **📖 How a Duel Works:** five short pages before a new save's first duel: life and lands, your turn (**End Turn ends
  your turn at once and skips your attack**), attacking and blocking, what a loss costs on your difficulty, and where
  Concede is. Read them again any time from **Settings** or from the duel's **Game menu**. On a phone they say "tap",
  on a PC "click". A save that has already fought doesn't see them.

### A new Quest Log

- **📜 Forge's new two-column journal:** story and side quests on the left, the quest's page on the right (one at a
  time in portrait), "New" tags, and finished stages struck out.
- **Everything TFR showed is still there:** pillaged towns and roaming legends under **On the Map** (a raid's page has
  its mini-map, and Track becomes **Show on Map**), side-quest timers, stage progress "(2/5)", hints and tips.

### A smarter AI

- **Allows for your combat tricks:** no "safe" block or attack into your open mana and a pump spell, and a team pump
  you've already cast (Inspired Charge) is counted before it blocks.
- **Better trades:** it trades a blocker to save its planeswalker, no longer throws creatures into trades at full life
  on Insane, and won't crack a fetchland into lethal.
- **Torment of Hailfire** waits until it can pay X=3 or more.
- **No more stalled duels:** the Life from the Loam (and Raise Dead) loop that froze a duel for minutes is gone; a free
  Equipment such as Shuko no longer hops back and forth all turn; Giant Koi and a copy spell aimed at a face-down spell
  no longer make the AI skip its move.
- **Big boards on Android:** the AI waits less on its turn when the board is huge (an Elf swarm).
- **Felidar Sovereign and Test of Endurance** are out of the enemy decks: Insane's big enemy life totals made them
  wins on the spot.

### Fixes

- **📱 Android: The War Begins** pages now show, and World Standings' **The War** no longer closes the game. A save
  that 1.29 marked as read without showing the pages shows them once.
- **💾 Continue after Data > Restore** no longer crashes, and a restore with a game loaded no longer lets Resume save
  the old game over the restored files.
- **⬇️ Android's resource download** asks you to keep the game on screen, and tells you when a download stopped.
- **➕ New Game+:** the Difficulty drop-down works again (its list opened behind the dialog). Moving up to Hard or Insane
  turns Mirror Gear on, moving down to Easy or Normal turns it off, and the dialog says so.
- **🗂️ The version backup** is made by the copy you keep playing when two copies are open.
- **🐉 The Magma Ogre** idles calmly, and a stray mark is gone from its death.

### Under the hood

- **Engine:** Forge's 11.18 build. Reward screens with many cards scroll instead of shrinking them, item text fits inside
  its card, and a large batch of AI and mana-payment improvements. Your adventure folder is backed up once on the
  first launch of 1.30.

**New game?** No. Everything in 1.30 works on your current save. The first-week grace counts from day 1 of a world, so
a save already past day 7 plays for ante as before.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.30-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi), and keep the game on screen
   until it finishes. The app restarts itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
