## The Forsaken Realms 1.26: Journals & Doppelgangers

- **📖 The Realm Journal:** a Journal on the character sheet for every creature and legend you meet this run: what you
  know of it, the decks you've fought, and which enemies will start with extra lands because of your win streak.
  Alt-click an enemy to open its page.
- **🎭 New race, the Doppelganger:** pick any four sets to start with (your difficulty decides how many start open), and
  take a new shape after every win.
- **🎲 Choose your starting sets** on the new-game screen.
- **🏛️ The golden Capitol:** new art for its Research Lab, Trader, Arenas, Armory, Bank, Exchange, Temple, Archaeologist,
  Spellsmith, Inn and Job Board, plus 3D mines in every town and five more caves with their own entrances.
- **🤖 Latest Forge engine, stronger AI:** enemies now plan around your next turn, so expect tougher duels on every
  difficulty. Rules and crash fixes come with it.
- **🗺️ Quests and raids:** raids get a Details page with a mini-map, Alt plates mark raiders and quest targets, quest
  cards keep their hint, a quest's reputation goes to the right town, and a side quest you take again gets its full
  time.
- **💰 Economy:** resource icons everywhere, a destroyed Bank refunds your deposits, overpriced cards like Channel are
  fixed, Found Cards can show only cards in your decks, the Research Lab never sells a copy your decks use, a shop pack
  never costs less than its set's packs sell for, and the Spellsmith charges before it shows the card. Landscape
  Sketchbooks are now one of each, so a chest or boss whose book you own pays less.
- **⚖️ Fair play:** reloading no longer re-rolls a chest (dungeon chests included), a diamond, a level-up offer or its
  payout (except what's inside a booster pack), or rewinds your win ratio.
- **🔁 Duel fixes:** a big batch of life-gain triggers no longer ends the duel in a draw, duels against Oura no longer
  crash, an AI attack can no longer freeze a duel, and the end-of-duel screen shows only once.
- **🛠️ Stability:** no more dead screen on an Android relaunch, no freeze from a failed reward card, fewer crashes in big
  duels, a clear warning if a save runs out of memory, a save that failed to load can't overwrite its good backup, a
  copied save file no longer crashes Save/Load, and a dungeon quest no longer blacks out its town.
- **🎚️ Difficulty and world:** the presets' random loot rate now reaches a new game (Insane's is 50%), day and night
  change terrain life on roads too, a new game in the same session no longer keeps the last world's bonfires, a map's
  special enemy no longer changes that creature everywhere, a dungeon's effect stays on its own level, defended towns
  field varied defenders, and a boss defeat cracks one item, not two.
- **📱 Android:** shorter AI waits on big boards, about 25 MB less memory held all session, the world map and History free their images when you leave them, and Settings fits in portrait.
- **🔒 Privacy:** Export Logs no longer includes your Windows user name.
- **🧹 Smaller fixes:** gold amounts over 1,000 show the coin, settings survive the app being closed mid-save, the
  Balance Sheet shows the week, Repair keeps your item selected, and the crash screen drops a Report button that sent
  nothing.
- The guide and the Discord FAQ are refreshed, and now say exactly how the Capitol is targeted, that the Arena pays half
  Power, and when Jumpstart stops being offered.

**New game?** Only to play the Doppelganger or pick your starting sets (New Game+ keeps your race and draws its sets at
random). Everything else works on your current save.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.26-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts
   itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
