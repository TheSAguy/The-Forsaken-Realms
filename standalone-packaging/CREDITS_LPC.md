# LPC character credits

The people-shaped enemies made from the **Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator** parts
(github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator, checkout ba4beebe6), composed with the mod's own port of the
generator's renderer and cut into the game's atlas format. Every part keeps its own license; the composed sprite carries the strictest
of them (CC-BY-SA where a share-alike part is in it). Per enemy: the parts drawn, their authors, the license used for each part, and the
sources.
