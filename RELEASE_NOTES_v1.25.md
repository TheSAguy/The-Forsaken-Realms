## The Forsaken Realms 1.25: Gauntlets & Wrath

- **🎚️ Custom Difficulty.**
  - "Advanced..." on the new-game screen: start from Easy, Normal, Hard or Insane and fine-tune 183 settings across 10
    tabs, with search and Basic/Expert views.
  - ⚠️ Be aware: there are a lot of settings, and some combinations can break the game. Change a few at a time, and use
    Reset all to get back to your preset.
  - Change settings mid-run in Settings > Difficulty > Edit... (new-game-only settings are locked).
  - Share codes.
  - New Game+ Advanced...
  - Your difficulty is shown on the info page, every save slot and in Settings.
  - New Game+ keeps your save's difficulty (it used to drop to Normal).
  - Disable Notoriety, leveling and the reload promise are kept per save and follow your Settings change.
- **🏰 18 Gauntlet dungeons:**
  - three Apprentices, two Adepts and one Master in a row, behind sealed gates;
  - themed enemy groups that change each appearance;
  - every dungeon gets its own entrance (plus 15 caves);
  - a quest that brings a dungeon's enemies back also closes its gates.
- **⚔️ The Temple's Wrath:**
  - build a Temple, and the color you've killed most hunts you the next week: its lands spawn tougher enemies, and its
    foes pay a +25% bounty;
  - a Wrath mage walks out to find you, and is never eased;
  - beat it for a prize and the "Wrath Breaker" title.
- **😈 Notoriety and reloading:**
  - a Settings switch, "I won't cheat by loading after a loss" (on by default);
  - on Hard and Insane, each cheat (reloading after a loss, or quitting mid-duel) gives enemies +1 life, up to +25.
    Every 3 losses you take honestly removes one. Arena losses never count;
  - a counted reload or mid-duel quit costs that loss's Notoriety; a quit after an error dialog never counts;
  - the Notoriety deck card is an extra card, from 50/45/40 wins by difficulty (sooner at a 95% win rate);
  - at Notoriety 50 the last bonus Wall becomes one of the enemy's own cards;
  - the info page shows Notoriety, with wins and losses on one line.
- **🧙 Legends:** Sanar, Memnarch, Gwendlyn Di Corci, Torsten Von Ursus, Alhammarret and Sandwurm play real decks built
  around themselves (some brought almost nothing but lands). Sanar's life drops to 15.
- **🛒 Shops and research:**
  - a Booster Shop under the Capitol's Card Shop (packs from all your unlocked sets); the era booster shops are gone
    (old saves convert);
  - a shop card never costs less than it sells for;
  - restricted cards (the Power Nine, Sol Ring...) never appear in any booster pack;
  - the Research Lab gets a set filter, a found-card count, a Sort box (Found / A-Z / Days, remembered), and Found Cards
    with the deck builder's color, type and rarity filters and group-by;
  - the research quest explains that selling or sacrificing a set's cards speeds its research;
  - the Inn's False Life charges the price it shows.
- **💾 Safer saves:**
  - a crash, or closing the app mid-save, can no longer leave an empty save;
  - a cut-off save loads the one before it (marked "recovered" in Save/Load) and doesn't count as a cheat;
  - saving over a damaged slot keeps the good backup.
- **🗺️ World and quality of life:**
  - hold Alt to see how long a cave has left;
  - no chest or diamond starts a duel in week 1;
  - Far Sight is +1 tile per pick;
  - blueprints from the first minute;
  - a dungeon's upgrades are fixed per visit (a reload won't re-roll them);
  - New Game+ starts clean (the job boards reset, and your Homeward rune comes back worn);
  - locked gates and barriers can no longer be walked through by bumping them;
  - talk-only characters no longer trap you in dialog;
  - the Mages' Fort clears only once its ward wizards are down;
  - the Bronze Coin and Auto-Sell buttons show their full text;
  - Android: real download sizes, and portrait fixes for the info page and Save/Load.
- **🔧 Updated to the latest official Forge.**
- The guide and the Discord FAQ are refreshed for all of the above.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.25-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts
   itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
