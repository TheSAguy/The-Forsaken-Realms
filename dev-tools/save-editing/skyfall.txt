# Skyfall (G_R) - round 148b, slot index 2 ("Slot 3" in the UI).
#
# Built to beat flier decks, NOT to race them in the air. Reach costs far less per point of stats
# than flying (Giant Spider 2/4 for 4 vs Charging Griffin 2/2 for 4), so ten reach creatures wall
# their whole air force while the fat ones walk in on the ground - which is where a flier deck has
# no defense at all. Green carries the flier-specific removal; red is a splash for instant-speed
# answers and Electrickery's one-sided sweep.
#
# The three cards that do the most work:
#   Spitting Spider   - "Sacrifice a land: deals 1 damage to EACH creature with flying." Repeatable,
#                       one-sided, and my own creatures never take a point of it.
#   Arbor Colossus    - 6/6 reach, and monstrosity destroys their best flier and makes it a 9/9.
#   Katabatic Winds   - while it is phased in, creatures with flying can't attack OR block.
