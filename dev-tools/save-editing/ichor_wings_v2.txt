# Ichor Wings v2 - save 1 slot 1, mono-black toxic (2026-09-20)
#
# The v1 list had singletons of cards the collection holds three and four of, and left the best
# black card in the save - Vraska, Betrayal's Sting - sitting unused. This is the same deck, played
# to its full copies: the poison clock runs on cheap toxic bodies, Karumonix pumps the rats, and
# everything expensive is either removal that proliferates or a 6/6 that costs four.
#
# Corrupted (Bonepicker Skirge, Ravenous Necrotitan) wants three poison on the opponent early, which
# is what the one-drops and Ichor Rats' "each player gets a poison counter" are there to guarantee.
#
# Cut from v1: Prosthetic Injector x2 (a +0/+2 equipment is slower than another toxic body),
# Pestilent Syphoner x2 and Plague Stinger (1/1 fliers, outclassed by Bonepicker Skirge).
