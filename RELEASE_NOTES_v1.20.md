## The Forsaken Realms — v1.20 — Power & Plunder

This is one of the bigger releases, especially for how the game plays and works. Adding XP/Levels (Ascendance) and
changing how companions work will have a huge effect on game play. Let me know how it goes, and please share your game
logs with me: `%APPDATA%\ForsakenRealms\forge.log`.

**You must start a new game for all the new features!** Your v1.19 save still loads, but Ascendance comes
only with a new game or New Game+.

## New

- **Ascendance, your character's level:**
  - Earn Power from duels, quests, first visits to places, chests and diamonds. After a duel, Power is a card on the
    loot screen.
  - Most levels let you pick 1 of 3 rewards: lasting perks (Haggler, Swift Feet, Shardwell, Mechanic, Medic...) or
    one-time gold, shards, wood, stone or cards. Don't like the three? Re-roll them once per level for shards.
  - Milestone levels give max life, more main-item slots and titles.
  - The HUD shows your progress to the next level, and the Level sheet lists every pick.
  - Harder difficulties level more slowly. Switch it off in Settings if you prefer.
- **Lost-treasure hunts:**
  - Six hunts per world: the Wastes and the five colors.
  - Each region's obelisk gives one map piece a week. Arenas and the Archaeologist give fragments too, and the
    Cartographer's Compass shows every obelisk.
  - Dig with the Spade (5 shards a dig). A guarded treasure waits under the X.
- **Castle keys:** the five AI castles open only with their color's Castle Key (one of that region's treasures) or
  when you're at War with that color.
- **Towns being pillaged:**
  - A random weekly event near dungeon-heavy areas.
  - Kill 5 raiders within a week for +1 reputation, 50 wood and 50 stone. Fail and you lose 2 reputation. Roaming
    guards pitch in.
- **The companion limit:** one creature-starting item at a time; two at Ascendance 15, three at 25.

## Changed

- **Mana items:**
  - Common mana and land items are now Uncommon.
  - The 15 Left, Right and Boots mana items move to the Neck slot, with new pendant icons.
  - They crack half as often on Easy and Normal, and four are renamed.
- **Story places:** the Strixhaven classrooms and ten more leave once cleared.
- **Invasions and ranks:**
  - Invasion leaders are one rank above their troops, with a Gemstone Mine on Hard and Insane.
  - The Elves and Bandits get an Archmage; the praetors and Arzakon are now Archmages.
- The Armory shows item descriptions.

## Engine

Forge 2.0.16 (the 2026-10-07 daily), the same as v1.19.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.20-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~210 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
