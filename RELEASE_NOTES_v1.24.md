## The Forsaken Realms — v1.24 — Towns & Trophies

Your towns take a trade, your realm earns lasting boons, research gets a new Lab, and TFR has its own achievements.
The engine moves to Forge's 10.26 build.

**New game recommended:** town specialties, realm milestones and Research Lab v2 need a new game or New Game+.
Everything else works on your v1.23 save.

## Headline features

- **Town specialties.** Every town you restore picks a trade: Mining, Research, Outpost, Smithy, Garrison or Market,
  each with its own bonus and a badge on the World Map (Names view). *New games and New Game+.*
- **Realm milestones.** Every 10 towns you hold at once, choose 1 of 3 lasting realm boons: masteries of your
  specialties, a Tithe from every town, Town Watch, Lamplighters, or a free Royal Grant research. *New games and NG+.*
- **Research Lab v2.** Each set has its own research, shortened by selling or sacrificing that set's cards. The Lab
  shows days, not a meter, and a Found Cards grid lets you sell or sacrifice spares right there (or "Sell all over 4").
  *New games and NG+.*
- **TFR's own achievements.** 16 new achievements (capitals, castles, legends, Grand Circuit, Notorious...), announced
  with an "Achievement Earned" card showing the trophy's art.
- **Six new enemies,** art by @xandghost: the Torchhead Stallion, Ashbark Gaunt, Thornspine Ravager, Bogmaw Golem,
  Flamecrest Serpent and Crocodile Hexmage, each as an Apprentice, an Adept and a Master.
- **Enemy info plates.** Hold Alt to see what you know of every enemy on screen: level always, and deck colors and life
  once you have fought that kind.
- **Engine update to Forge's 10.26 daily.** New Star Trek cards, many card fixes, and better AI mulligans.

## Gameplay and balance

- Notoriety: a lost duel takes 5 off instead of resetting it, and Settings has a new "Disable Notoriety" option (no
  Walls, no stronger foes).
- Arena: each tournament you win this week puts an Archmage in your other brackets, and last week's wins thin out the
  lowest tier. Arena duels pay half Power.
- Reloading right after a lost duel is noticed: the Notices log teases you, and on Hard and Insane enemies gain +1 life
  per reload (at most +10). Quitting mid-duel counts as a loss.
- Difficulty hints: losing to the same foe 5 times suggests an easier difficulty, and 50 wins in a row a harder one,
  for your next game or New Game+.
- No defeat gold minimum in week 1: a lost duel in the world's first week takes only the percentage.
- An endless-loop breaker (with a menu option after 10 repeats), and a draw is no longer a loss.
- Each color capital sells its +1 max life only once per game.
- Witch-Maw Slippers cost two opening-hand cards, and drop only from the Akroma and Lathliss castle bosses, one pair per
  player.
- The Demon's Bargain and the Sphinx's Sanctum open in week 3. World-map enemies walk around obstacles.

## Quality of life and UI

- A "Show land drops in duels" option shows the lands played and allowed this turn for each player.
- Always Yes / Always No now work for Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle and Spark Mage.
- On PC, the start screen tells you when a newer version is out, with a Download page button.
- The build menu opens at once, towns load faster, and Card Shop rows show their card count.
- Teleporter mini-maps show where each destination sends you. F4 works in story maps; only a chase locks it.
- The Notices log is saved and catches more (arena wins, reputation, color standing); resource pickups go to the log
  instead of a banner.
- The Gear Sets page lists your best sets first.

## Fixes

- No banned card (Sol Ring, the Power 9...) can be won from an enemy's deck or ante any more; cards already owned stay.
- Tarnation, the Wizard Palace and the Church of Valgavoth lead to their deeper floors again.
- Fallen colors' level places appear; quest kills count for more troop kinds; land shops sell Snow-Covered basics again.
- An AI crash over regeneration, Grolnok's Skin, quest text, and the Vampire hero's bat flight are fixed.
- Android: leaving the app mid-duel is judged at the moment you left, so time in the background no longer counts as
  quitting.
- Behind the scenes: shared logs carry short summary lines, which make bug reports easier to read.

## New game needed?

- **Need a new game (or New Game+):** town specialties, realm milestones, and Research Lab v2 (with Found Cards and
  the days view). Older saves keep classic research and never see a specialty or a boon.
- **Work on existing saves:** everything else, including achievements (back-filled from your save), the new enemies,
  Alt plates, the Notoriety changes, the arena rules and every fix.
- **Returning players should know:** a loss now costs 5 Notoriety rather than resetting it; arena wins pay half Power;
  a capital's heart sells once per game (an old save bought in an earlier week may sell one more); new worlds lay out
  differently from the same seed (the engine now places side-boss lairs with the caves); research in a v2 game takes
  40 days with nothing fed and about 3 sacrificed boosters to finish.

feature.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.24-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts
   itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
