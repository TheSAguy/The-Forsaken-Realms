## The Forsaken Realms 1.27: Bounties & Gold

### New things to do

- **💰 The Bounty:** beat an enemy that started with Notoriety Walls, its own Notoriety card, streak Wastes or help
  tokens and the win pays more gold and resources (up to +30%). Hold Alt to see it on the enemy's plate.
- **🏛️ The golden Capitol** gets its own Card Shop, Booster Shop, Outlook tower and an animated Teleporter, and its
  buildings are recolored to match the Orazca icon.
- **🍺 Inns deal smarter events:** Draft and Sealed draw from the town's color, picks are spread evenly, and nearby Inns
  don't repeat each other's sets.
- **🎚️ Change your difficulty level mid-game:** Settings > Difficulty > Edit... > Change level.
- **🎵 Added some more music tracks.**

### Fixes you'll notice

- **🧠 Memory:** big token boards no longer run phones out of memory, and long sessions stop piling up memory (duel card
  art and enemy sprite sheets are freed again).
- **📱 Android:** a crash outside a duel shows a message and closes cleanly instead of freezing the screen.
- **🔁 Duels:** a duel crash from a timer race is fixed.
- **⚖️ Fair play:** reloads no longer re-roll quest rewards, cave champions, Inn packs, the Temple's offering, realm
  boons or a dungeon's re-theme.
- **🗺️ Dungeons and quests:** a dungeon win's result can't land on the wrong enemy or map; a Job Board offer you decline
  no longer resets its dungeon; Emrakul's Castle and the Temples of Chandra and Liliana stay hidden until the story
  opens them (since 1.0 they had opened for everyone after any load), and the relic-trail soft-lock is gone.
- **🏪 Shops:** AI towns keep their shop types (on a current save some change once, then stay fixed).
- **⚙️ Autonomous Factory:** only one fixed Clay Golem per map, the booster guard; the others roll their rank by the
  week.
- **🔄 Settings' Restart** saves first, or warns what will be lost.
- **🚫 The chest's traveling artificer** never copies a banned card such as Sol Ring; copies you already own stay.
- **🖼️ Alchemy cards** show their picture again (the original printed card, so its text can differ from the rebalanced
  rules the game plays).
- **🔤 The clean font** now covers checkboxes, lists and dialog titles, and a game launched minimized gets the right font
  size.

### Under the hood

- **Engine:** the newest Forge daily (the 11.05 build).
- **Better bug reports:** new log lines for what fills memory, whether the last PC session closed cleanly, console
  commands and shop sales.

**New game?** Not needed: everything in 1.27 works on your current save.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.27-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~430 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts
   itself when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
