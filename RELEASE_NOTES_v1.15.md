## The Forsaken Realms — v1.15 — Cobblestone & Crown

Your v1.14 save loads and is updated on the first load (roads are redrawn once).

## New

- **Your own roads:** once the Capitol stands, cobblestone roads join it to every town you hold. You travel faster on
  them, and the old dirt roads beneath are cleaned up.
- **A new Capitol and a new town layout** for the towns you restore. Ruins keep the old look until you rebuild them,
  and Orazca shows the Warden in its restored form.
- **Mythic shops** in the Capitol, and one blueprint price per shop type, set by its tier in your own build menu.
- **Fog zones:** what spawns, how strong and how fast, now depends on the fog where it appears.
- **Legends** stay for three days and show in your quest log.
- **New items:** the Bonfire (lifts the fog around you) and the Yin-Yang Rune (drop one half, return to it later),
  both found in Armories, plus a second utility slot.
- **Five ruins** now serve as dungeon entrances, and the Demon's Bargain awaits in the wilds.
- **Hostile colors go for your Capitol:** at Unhappy or War it is more likely to be picked, with a chance of a
  stronger mage.
- **The endgame:** a fallen Lord's Ring Cities go back to working neutral cities, and when the last Lord falls every
  Ring City opens its gates to you.
- A FAQ ships in the game folder, and the player guide is updated.

## Fixed

- Roads doubled up or ran past towns they should have passed through.
- Popups on the world map no longer let time run underneath them (a Capitol defense could be skipped).
- A defeated color's Ring City came back as a ruin you could buy for a restore fee.
- Walls, gates, trees and shrines that blocked your way in the Capitol and towns.
- The Warden greeted you as if Orazca were still in ruins.

## 📱 Android

**Install:**
1. On your Android device (Android 8.0+), download `forsaken-realms-1.15-signed-aligned.apk` from the assets below.
2. Tap the downloaded file and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when prompted (the game is
   signed by us, not the Play Store).
3. Grant the storage permission the app asks for; it stores the game data it downloads.
4. On first launch, tap **Download** when offered the resource files (~210 MB, use Wi-Fi). The app restarts itself
   when finished. After that it plays offline.
5. It works alongside the official Forge app. Updating over an earlier version keeps your saves.

`assets.zip` in the file list is downloaded by the app automatically; you don't need it yourself. Problems or
cut-off layouts: Discord (https://discord.gg/TTRPKc9HYJ) with your device model.
